## Remaining Limitations

These gaps were preserved from the supplied sheet, not filled by inference:
222 blank Grains, 5 blank Addresses, 99 blank URLs, 63 blank Sources,
148 blank Phones, and 225 blank Emails. No Verified record lacks a Source.
Functions are complete, but completeness does not prove factual accuracy.

603 map points are street-address geocodes, 109 are place-level, 1 is an
organization match, and 16 are state-level approximations. Approximate locations
remain explicitly labeled in the app; geocoding does not establish ownership
or operation at an address.

Two A-Frame Farm records in Minnesota retain distinct addresses and IDs.
They remain a possible duplicate requiring factual review, not an automatic
deletion. Upper Crust Baking retains both Wheat and Khorasan Wheat as supplied.

The local release archive includes the original and cleaned exports, the previous
public dataset, a field-change log, and fingerprinted exceptions recording these
limitations. No paid search or LLM service was used.
